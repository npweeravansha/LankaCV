About LankaCV

Helping you present your skills with confidence.

A simple CV builder designed with Sri Lankan job seekers in mind.

Who We Are

LankaCV is an online CV-building tool created to help students, fresh graduates, and job seekers in Sri Lanka prepare a clear and professional-looking CV. Our goal is to make the CV creation process straightforward and accessible.

What We Offer

  • A simple form for entering personal and professional details.
  • Sections for education, work experience, skills, and other relevant information.
  • A CV preview so you can review your details before saving.
  • A browser-based print workflow that lets you save your CV as a PDF where supported.

Our Mission

We want to make it easier for people to prepare a CV and take the next step in their education or career. You can use LankaCV without creating an account.

Use the Information You Provide Carefully

Please enter accurate information and review your CV before sharing it with an employer. Your CV may contain sensitive personal details, so only share or upload it with people and services you trust.

Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or need help using the builder, contact us through WhatsApp Support.
