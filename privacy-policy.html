Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Please read how information is handled.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

LankaCV is designed to let you create a CV without registering for an account. Do not enter information you do not want included in your CV.

1. Information You Enter

When using the CV builder, you may enter details such as your name, contact information, address, date of birth, profile summary, education, employment history, skills, references, and profile photo. These details are used by the page to display your CV preview and help you print or save it.

2. How CV Information Is Handled

The current builder code processes the CV fields in your browser and does not require an account. We do not operate a CV account database through the builder code provided with this site. Your browser, extensions, device, or any third-party service you choose to use may handle information according to their own settings and policies. Avoid using a shared or public device for private information.

When you use the browser's print or “Save as PDF” feature, that process is handled by your browser or operating system. Check its settings if you have privacy concerns.

3. Hosting and Technical Information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request logs, and security-related data under its own policies. We do not control the hosting provider's infrastructure or retention practices.

4. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

The supplied website code does not itself require an account or use CV details for analytics. If analytics, advertising, embedded content, or other third-party services are added in the future, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies. This policy should be updated to identify those services before they are enabled.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell CV details entered into the current browser-based builder. You decide whether to download, print, or share the CV you create. Information may be processed by the hosting provider or by services you independently choose to use, subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security and Retention

We do not promise that any website or device is completely secure. Since CV details can include personal information, keep your device secure, avoid public computers where possible, and delete downloaded files from shared devices. Information may remain in browser memory, downloads, print systems, or device backups depending on your settings.

7. Children

LankaCV is intended for people preparing a CV for education or employment. If you are under the age required to provide consent under applicable law, use the site with guidance from a parent or guardian.

8. Your Choices

You can choose what information to enter and whether to save or share your CV. You may leave fields blank when they are not needed. If you have a privacy question, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us via WhatsApp Support.

This page is general website information and is not legal advice. Review it against the site's actual services and applicable law before relying on it as a legal policy.
